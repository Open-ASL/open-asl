# Credits

Open ASL stands on the work of a great many people in the Advanced Squad Leader community. Thank you.

## Map boards

The high-resolution map boards are the work of **Neal Ulen**, who drew them and kindly allows Open ASL
to use them. Find them, and much more, at **[asl-players.net](https://asl-players.net)**.

## VASL

The counter art, the marker art, and the line-of-sight and terrain data for every board come from
**[VASL](https://github.com/vasl-developers/vasl)** (Virtual Advanced Squad Leader), written by Rodney
Kinney and maintained by the VASL developers, with board and counter art by its many contributors. The
full VASL credits are reproduced in [`NOTICE.md`](NOTICE.md).

## Sound

The sound effects are CC0 recordings from [Freesound](https://freesound.org); each one's author is
listed in [`apps/web/src/audio/sounds/CREDITS.md`](apps/web/src/audio/sounds/CREDITS.md).

## Fonts and libraries

- Fonts: Black Ops One, Oswald and Barlow (SIL Open Font License), plus JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex and
  Newsreader, served by Google Fonts.
- The scenario atlas uses [MapLibre GL JS](https://maplibre.org) and the
  [world-atlas](https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas) country outlines from
  [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com).
- The board is drawn with [PixiJS](https://pixijs.com); the app is built with React, Vite and Firebase.
