# Medal art

The supreme decorations are shown as pictures of the medals themselves. Each picture was rendered as new game art by
an image model, working from the photographs of the real medals listed below, and then checked feature by feature
against them. Nothing was designed from scratch.

The Knight's Cross is its 1957 form, the version the German order law of 1957 permits: an oak-leaf cluster in the
centre of the cross, where the original bore a swastika. Its reference joined the two photographs listed for it: the
cross of the 1957 piece and the diamond-set oak leaves and swords of an original clasp, which never bore one.
